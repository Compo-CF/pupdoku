## Description
Meet Pupdoku — a cozy logic puzzle where you find the hidden puppies. Every board is split into colorful regions, and your job is to figure out exactly where each pup belongs. Easy to learn, wonderfully tricky to master, and stuffed with adorable illustrated breeds.

ONE PUP PER ROW, COLUMN & COLOR
Place one puppy in every row, every column, and every colored region — and make sure no two puppies ever touch, not even diagonally. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so it is always solvable with logic. No guessing required.

HOW YOU PLAY
Tap a square once to rule it out with an X, tap again to place a puppy, tap once more to clear it. Mark the squares you have eliminated to zero in on where each pup must go.

GROWS WITH YOU
- Start on gentle 5x5 boards, then unlock 6x6, 7x7, 8x8 and 9x9 as you win
- A fresh Daily Puzzle every day, with streaks
- Undo, Clear, and hints whenever you want them

MAKE IT YOURS
- Earn Bones for every solve and spend them your way
- Unlock and switch colorful board themes
- Seasonal Event Passes add festive themes and puppy costumes

PARADE PASS
Subscribe to the Parade Pass for ad-free play and unlimited hints while active.

FAIR AND FRIENDLY
- One-tap Remove Ads, and NO surprise full-screen ads during play — ever
- Optional hint packs, or watch an ad only if you choose to
- Game Center leaderboards and achievements
- iCloud sync across your devices, and full offline play
- Colorblind-friendly labels and a relaxed, no-fail feel

Whether you have two minutes or twenty, Pupdoku is a calm, rewarding brain game with a happy ending every time: a board full of puppies. Download and start your pack today!

## App Review Notes
No login or account is required — all features are accessible on launch.

WHAT IS NEW IN 3.0
This version adds a full in-app economy: a "Bones" currency, cosmetic board themes, seasonal Event Passes, a Starter Pack bundle, and a new auto-renewable subscription (Parade Pass).

HOW TO REACH EVERYTHING
- Tap Play to start a puzzle (5x5 is available immediately; larger sizes unlock after a few wins). The Daily Puzzle button always gives a full-size board.
- Home screen -> Shop shows all purchases.

SUBSCRIPTION — Parade Pass (com.centricfiber.pupdoku.paradepass.monthly, monthly)
- Unlocks ad-free play and unlimited hints while active.
- The paywall shows the price, what it unlocks, the auto-renew terms, and links to Terms (EULA) and Privacy. Restore Purchases is available in the Shop.

BONES & COSMETICS
- Bones are earned by solving puzzles and can also be purchased (Get Bones in the Shop).
- Spend Bones on hints, on board themes (Shop -> Cosmetics), or to unlock Event Passes.

EVENT PASSES & COSTUMES (how to see costumes)
- Buy the Spooky or Winter pass (via IAP or Bones) in the Shop.
- Then open Shop -> Cosmetics, select that theme. Placed puppies will then wear the seasonal costume (witch hat / santa hat).

ADS
- Google AdMob. Banner appears on the Home screen only (never during a puzzle). An occasional interstitial may appear between puzzles. A rewarded ad is optional ("watch for a hint"). Remove Ads (one-time) and the Parade Pass both remove ads.

Progress syncs privately via iCloud (CloudKit) and the game plays fully offline.
